## 2. Fixing a weak prompt

Fixed:
```
A frosted glass perfume bottle with a brushed-gold cap stands on wet black stone. Side backlight rims the bottle's edges; the background falls to soft blur and drifting mist. A woman's hand, seen only from the wrist down with a thin gold bracelet, lifts the bottle and sprays once; the mist catches the light as it floats across frame. 0–4s: macro on the bottle, slow lateral slide left. 4–8s: the hand enters and sprays. 8–10s: pull back to a centred hero shot of the bottle as the mist settles. What changed: "premium/luxury/epic" became lighting and material detail; "don't show the face" became a positive framing ("seen only from the wrist down"); added timeline and audio; resolution moved to settings.
```

## 4. Multiple keyframes narrative
Keyframes entry. Uploads: @Image1 empty attic, @Image2 boy finding a wooden box, @Image3 box open with light glowing, @Image4 attic transformed into a starry sky.
Settings: Keyframes · 20 s · aspect follows @Image1
```
Keyframes: @Image1 at 0s (empty dusty attic, boy at the doorway), @Image2 at 6s (boy kneeling beside a small carved wooden box), @Image3 at 12s (lid open, warm golden light on his face), @Image4 at 20s (the attic walls dissolved into a night sky full of stars, boy standing in the centre).

0–6s (@Image1 → @Image2): The boy steps in cautiously, floorboards creaking, dust drifting through a thin beam from the roof window. He notices the box under a sheet, pulls the sheet away and kneels. Slow push-in following him.
6–12s (@Image2 → @Image3): He brushes dust off the lid, hesitates, then lifts it. Light spills out and grows brighter on his face; his expression moves from curiosity to wonder. Camera holds a medium close-up.
12–20s (@Image3 → @Image4): Specks of light rise from the box and drift upward; the roof beams and walls fade into deep blue sky as stars appear one by one. He stands and turns slowly, looking up. Camera cranes up and pulls back to a wide shot.
Continuity: same boy, same striped sweater and messy brown hair, same box throughout. Change only the environment in the final segment.
Audio: creaking boards, soft dust sounds, a faint music-box melody that begins when the lid opens and swells gently to the end. No dialogue. No subtitles.
```

## 5. Long-form brief, dialogue scene
Text only. Settings: Omni Reference · 15 s · 16:9 · 1080p
```
1. SCENE CONTEXT: EXACTLY 2 CHARACTERS — NO DUPLICATES. A Japanese grandmother and her British grandson cook together in a small Kyoto kitchen. He tries to speak Japanese; she answers in English. 15 seconds, three shots, warm and gentle pacing.

2. ACTIVE REFERENCES: None. Grandmother: about 75, short grey hair, navy apron over a cream cardigan. Grandson: about 20, curly red hair, green sweater with rolled sleeves. Keep both appearances identical in every shot.

3. LOCATION MAP: Gas stove on screen-left, wooden counter centre with a cutting board, window on screen-right. Grandmother stands at the stove, grandson at the counter. Camera stays on the window side throughout.

4. FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING: The grandson is already chopping green onions unevenly; the grandmother stirs a small pot of miso soup and glances at his cutting.

5. FORMAT MODE: 15 s, three hard cuts.
SHOT 1 — 0–5s: two-shot, he speaks.
SHOT 2 — 5–10s: close-up on her, she replies.
SHOT 3 — 10–15s: two-shot, shared laugh.

6. OPTICS: 35mm equivalent for two-shots, 65mm for her close-up. Shallow depth of field; steam and window light soft in the background.

7. CAMERA: Handheld with gentle breathing sway, small reframes, no fast moves.

8. ACTION TIMING:
0–1s: He stops chopping and looks up hopefully.
1–4s: He says his line carefully, slightly proud.
5–6s: She tastes the soup, then smiles at him.
6–9s: She answers, pointing at his uneven onion pieces with her spoon.
10–15s: He looks at the board, laughs; she laughs and pats his arm. He holds up one huge onion piece.

9. PHYSICS: Steam rises and bends toward the window draft. The knife rests on the board with a small clack.

10. LIGHTING: Soft late-afternoon daylight from the window on screen-right is the only key; warm tungsten bulb above as a practical.

11. AUDIO: Grandson speaks slow, careful Japanese with a British accent, warm and slightly nervous: "おばあちゃん、これでいい？" Pause 0.5 s. Grandmother answers in gentle, amused English with a Japanese accent, unhurried: "Good. But very big pieces." Emphasize "very big". One speaker at a time. Simmering pot, knife on wood, distant street sounds through the window. No background music. No subtitles.

12. CHARACTER ACTING: He wants her approval; his eyes flick between her and the board. She is fond and teasing; her smile starts before she speaks.

13. STYLE: Grounded, intimate slice-of-life, natural colour, subtle film grain. Palette: warm wood and cream 60%, steam-white 30%, green onion and sweater green 10%.

14. QUALITY: Consistent faces, hair and clothes; hands hold the knife and spoon correctly; lip sync matches each language.

15. POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS: Exactly two people. He speaks only Japanese; she speaks only English. Exactly two spoken lines. Everything happens in the one kitchen. No extra characters, subtitles, captions or music.
```

## 7. Stylized (clay stop-motion)
Text only. Settings: 10 s · 16:9
```
Handmade clay stop-motion with visible fingerprints, slightly uneven surfaces and stepped 12 fps motion. A small round clay owl in a knitted scarf tries to fly off a felt branch, flaps hard, rises a few centimetres and plops back down, then tries again and finally glides to a lower branch. Miniature-set lighting from a warm lamp on frame-left, shallow macro depth of field, felt night sky with tin-foil stars. Camera locked off with one small push-in at the final landing. Squeaky clay flaps, a soft thud, a tiny triumphant hoot. Fully handcrafted look, not photorealistic. No background music. No subtitles.
```
